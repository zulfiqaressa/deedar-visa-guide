Step 1

Gather your documents

Start now — do not wait for the facilitation letter to begin on the others.

A — Enrollment certificate

A

Get your enrollment certificate from the Admin block

Go to the Admin block at your university. The lady next to Zeqrija's office issues it once you hand over your student ID. Bring original + 1 copy.

Bring your student ID card

B — Bank account statement

B

Go to your bank and request a bank statement and account certificate

Must be from a traditional bank — Komercijalna Banka, Stopanska Banka, etc. Wise, Payoneer, and Revolut are not accepted.

Statement must cover the past 3–6 months. If in Macedonian, get it notary-translated into German or English.

Collecting money from friends? Deposit and wait at least 24–48 hours before taking the statement. Same-day deposit and statement will not work.

No Wise / Payoneer / Revolut
How much do I need in my account?
At least €500 should be in your account while applying.

C — Residence permit

C

Locate your North Macedonia residence permit

Original + 1 copy. Must be valid at least 1 month after your return date from Germany. If expiring before that, renew it first.

D — Passport photo

D

Get 1 biometric passport photo taken

White background · 45×35mm · Not older than 6 months · Neutral expression, face centred, no glasses. Ask any photo shop for a "biometric visa photo".

1 photo only — not 2

E — VIDEX visa application form (the main form)

E

Fill in the VIDEX form online — this is the actual visa application

VIDEX is the official German visa application. Fill it in online, then print, sign by hand, and bring the signed copy to your appointment. All other documents below are supporting attachments to this form.

G — Travel insurance

G

Travel health insurance — minimum €30,000 Schengen coverage

Must cover the full duration of your stay and be valid across all Schengen countries. Original + 1 copy.

Easiest option in Tetovo: Walk into Sava Osiguruvanje — done in about 5 minutes. Approximate prices:

7 days ≈ 700–900 MKD 14 days ≈ 1,100–1,400 MKD 30 days ≈ 1,800–2,400 MKD

Triglav and Euroins in Tetovo also offer the same. Confirm exact price at the counter. Make sure the policy states €30,000 minimum Schengen coverage.

Walk in · 5 minutes · done

H — Proof of accommodation in Germany

H

Book your accommodation near the Deedar venue — without this you cannot proceed with your application

The Deedar venue is Messe Berlin. Start looking for hostels and hotels nearby now so you have a confirmed booking to submit with your visa application.

You need either: a confirmed hotel/hostel booking for your full stay, or a formal invitation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung) from your host in Germany confirmed by the local German Aliens Authority.

Search hotels near Messe Berlin on Booking.com ↗ Recommended hostel — ~€17/night, central Berlin, easy U8 access ↗
Required before you can apply — do not delay

I — Visa facilitation letter

I

Being arranged by the council — expected today or tomorrow

The visa facilitation letter will be shared with everyone in the group as soon as it is received. This is the final document you need before you can submit your full application.

Do not wait for this letter to start on everything else. Have all other documents ready so you can move immediately once it arrives.

Coming today or tomorrow — have everything else ready
Translation required: All Macedonian documents must be officially translated into German or English by a certified notary translator before submission. Untranslated documents may result in rejection.

Process

Step-by-step process

Follow this order once your documents are in hand.

1

Gather all documents this week (A–I above)

Start immediately. Do not wait for the facilitation letter — have everything else ready before it arrives.

2

Book your accommodation and flights

You cannot submit your application without a confirmed accommodation booking. A recommended hostel is around €17/night — clean, central, with easy U8 access to get anywhere in Berlin. Also book your flights early — Wizz Air flies Skopje to Berlin for the Deedar dates.

Search Booking.com ↗Recommended hostel (~€17/night) ↗Wizz Air — SKP to BER ↗
3

Book your embassy appointment — deadline June 5

Visit the German Embassy Skopje page to book. Walk-in also accepted Monday–Friday 8:00–9:00am.

German Embassy Skopje ↗
4

Receive your facilitation letter

The council will share it in the group today or tomorrow. This is your final document before submission.

5

Submit your complete application

~15 working days processing + 2–3 days passport return. Fee: €90 adults · €45 children (6–12) · Free under 6.
